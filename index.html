2

Set up booking

Watch this part
  • Members book inside your Skool. Everyone else books from the link in your bio. Start with one route.
  • Both routes ask the same three questions. Their answers are what you prepare from.
The intake, both routes
  1. Where are you now?
  2. Where do you want to be in 90 days?
  3. What's your biggest challenge in getting there?
3

Prepare and run the session

Watch this part
  • Prepare the page from their intake: where they are, where they want to be, the one challenge, three moves. On the call they pick one move and you go deep on that one.
  • Around minute 45, recap and ask permission: "Did you want to continue this on your own, or would you like to hear what it would be like for us to work together?" Only then the 90-day offer, one price, said one way.
